7. DISCUSSION (draft for the report)
==================================================================================
L1 gives exact zeros because of the optimality condition: a coefficient can sit at
zero only while the correlation of its feature with the residual stays within lambda
(|Phi_j^T r| <= lambda), and the corners of the l1 ball (diamond) lie on the axes, where the
SSE contours touch first. The coefficient path shows the coefficients shrinking and then
switching off one after another (0 of 10 are ~0 at lambda = 1e-04, 8 of 10 at
lambda = 1). Ridge's circular l2 ball is touched away from the axes, so it shrinks every
coefficient without setting any to zero: at its best lambda 1 of 10 Ridge coefficients have
|w_j| < 0.01, against 4 of 10 for Lasso. Past lambda* = 1.0e-01, the penalty starts to dominate: train
RMSE rises monotonically (0.075 -> 0.346 at lambda = 1) and test RMSE rises too
(0.099 -> 0.348), i.e. the model underfits.
