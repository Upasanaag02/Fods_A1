==============================================================================
4. DISCUSSION (draft for the report)
==============================================================================
More data raise the posterior precision S_N^-1 = alpha I + beta Phi^T Phi (each new point adds
beta phi phi^T), so the posterior covariance S_N shrinks and the parameter-uncertainty term
phi(x)^T S_N phi(x) falls: the mean of that term drops from 0.0060 at N = 5 to 0.00075 at
N = 50, and the interval narrows from an average width of 0.495 to 0.406.
For N = 10 the band is narrowest near x = 0.49 and widest at x = 1.00. The noise term
beta^-1 = 0.01 is a property of the observations, not of the weights, so it does not depend
on N: the width can only approach the floor 0.392 and never go below it, however much data
we collect.
