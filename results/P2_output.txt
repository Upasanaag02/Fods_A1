5. DISCUSSION (draft for the report)
==============================================================================
Ridge is the MAP estimate under a zero-mean Gaussian prior, with lambda = alpha/beta.
The test-RMSE minimum is FLAT: 9 grid points lie within 5% of the best (0.115), spanning
lambda from 3.2e-08 to 1.0e-04 (about 3.5 decades), and over that whole span test RMSE
only moves between 0.115 and 0.122. Below the interval the fit overfits (train
RMSE -> 0, huge weights, wiggles between the points). Above it the penalty dominates and
the fit underfits (weights shrink towards 0, the curve is too smooth, and both train and
test RMSE rise).
